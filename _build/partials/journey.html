Our design process

The journey of your building.

Eight stages from first conversation to handover — each with a clear deliverable you review and approve.

  1. 01Consultation & brief

    Your goals, plot, budget and timeline — understood before anything is drawn.

  2. 02Architectural floor plan

    Room layouts, circulation and the structural grid planned together.

  3. 033D space layout

    See your spaces in 3D and refine proportions, light and flow.

  4. 04Structural drawings

    Analysis, foundations, columns, beams and slabs — fully detailed.

  5. 053D elevation design

    Facade, materials and lighting visualised before construction.

  6. 06Approval drawings

    Drawing sets prepared to support your statutory approvals.

  7. 073D interior design

    Interiors, joinery, finishes and lighting resolved room by room.

  8. 08Construction & handover

    Supervised execution, quality checks and a building ready to use.
