Our company

We are architects, engineers and builders working as one team — so the building you imagine is the building you receive.

SUBHA INFRA delivers architecture, interior design, structural engineering, steel and PEB structures, facades, tensile structures and complete design & build — for homes, commercial buildings, industrial facilities and special structures.

Our work begins long before construction. We study how a building will be used, how it will stand, how it will weather and how it will be built. That understanding shapes every drawing we issue.

Because design, engineering and execution sit within the same team, decisions are made with the full picture in view. Structural systems are resolved alongside the architecture. Materials are selected with fabrication and maintenance in mind. Site teams build from drawings they helped make buildable.

Our approach

Architecture and engineering, resolved together.

Two disciplines that are usually separated — and the reason so many buildings fall short of their drawings.

Architecture

Space first, then form.

We start with the brief, the site and the way people will move through a building. Form, facade and material follow from that — never the other way round. The result is architecture that is distinctive because it is appropriate.

Engineering

Structure as part of the design.

Our engineers join at concept stage. Grids, spans, cantilevers and foundations are considered while the architecture is still flexible, so ambitious ideas become efficient structures rather than expensive compromises.

Integrated capability

Design to execution, under one roof.

Engage us for one stage or for the entire journey. Each stage hands over cleanly to the next because the same team owns both.
