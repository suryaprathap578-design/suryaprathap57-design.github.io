Service 08

Tensile & special structures.

Light, expressive structures for shade, shelter and arrival — engineered for wind, weather and decades of use.

Overview

Form found by forces.

A tensile structure gets its strength from shape and pre-tension. Getting that shape right — together with masts, cables, anchors and drainage — is an engineering exercise as much as a design one.

We also design special steel and glass structures, such as raking car-park canopies and entrance structures, where the support system itself becomes the architecture.
