Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information SUBHA INFRA ("we", "us") collects through www.subhainfra.com, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry details you provide: name, company, phone, email, project location, project information, budget range and any files you choose to share.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information: third-party services embedded in this website, such as Google Fonts and Google Maps, may receive your IP address and browser information when pages load.

How enquiries are sent

Depending on how the website is configured, your enquiry is either sent to us through a form-handling service, or prepared for you to send by email or WhatsApp. If you send it by email or WhatsApp, the privacy terms of your email provider or WhatsApp also apply.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare proposals and deliver services you engage us for.
  • To keep records of communications and agreements.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only where necessary to deliver services — for example with consultants or partners involved in your project — or where required by law.

Retention

We keep enquiry and project information for as long as needed for the purposes above and for our legal and record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@subhainfra.com. We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. Embedded third-party services may set cookies under their own policies. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated.
